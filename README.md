# AI Impact Assessments

This repository showcases how I approach AI impact assessments for real-world systems, with a focus on governance, risk, and privacy. The examples are synthetic, but they are designed to reflect the kinds of challenges organizations face when deploying AI.

## What’s in this repo

- General AI impact assessment template
- Privacy-aware impact assessment template
- Case studies for different domains (finance, healthcare, HR, customer support)

## My focus

I’m transitioning into AI governance and AI risk roles, with a specialization in impact assessments. My work is informed by:

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- Emerging AI regulations (including the EU AI Act)
- Data protection and privacy principles

## For recruiters and hiring managers

This repo is meant to demonstrate how I:

- Identify and analyze potential harms and risks from AI systems
- Consider privacy, fairness, safety, and regulatory compliance together
- Structure impact assessments in a way that can be used in consulting and enterprise environments
